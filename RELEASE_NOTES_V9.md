# Release Notes V9 — Complete IELTS Academic Practice

## New form

Added `EN-IELTS-ACADEMIC-PRACTICE` with KLI-original content aligned to the current IELTS Academic structure:

- Listening: 4 parts, 40 objective items, approximately 30 minutes.
- Academic Reading: 3 passages, 40 objective items, 60 minutes.
- Academic Writing: 2 tasks, 60 minutes.
- Speaking: 3 parts, 11–14 minutes.

## Delivery and scoring

- Listening is played once using a fixed British-English voice profile for practice delivery.
- Transcripts are withheld while the test is active.
- Reading passages appear once before their question groups.
- Writing Task 1 requires at least 150 words; Task 2 requires at least 250 words.
- Speaking Parts 1–3 require recorded responses and human rating.
- Objective section scores and human-review workflow are preserved.

## Rights and claims

Cambridge IELTS 5 is stored outside the public directory as a licensed structural reference based on user attestation. Live questions, passages, prompts, and scripts are KLI-original. Results are KLI readiness evidence, not official IELTS bands.

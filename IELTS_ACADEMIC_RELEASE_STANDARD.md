# KLI Academic English Four-Skills Practice

A complete KLI-original IELTS Academic preparation form aligned to the current four-section structure and informed by the licensed Cambridge IELTS 5 reference.

- Listening: 4 parts, 40 items, approximately 30 minutes, one play.
- Academic Reading: 3 passages, 40 items, 60 minutes.
- Academic Writing: Task 1 (minimum 150 words) and Task 2 (minimum 250 words), 60 minutes.
- Speaking: 3 parts, 11–14 minutes, recorded for human rating.

The form reports KLI readiness evidence, not an official IELTS band. Browser speech delivery is acceptable for commercial practice. Studio audio, examiner calibration, pilot data, and standard setting are mandatory before validated high-stakes use.

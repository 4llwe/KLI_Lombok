# Release V5 — Academic Assessment Release Candidate

## Academic content
- 23 assessment forms and 460 original objective items after migrations.
- 17 level descriptors and 86 multi-skill tasks.
- English CEFR A1–C2, Japanese JLPT N5–N1 with clearly separated KLI productive extension, and German CEFR A1–C2.
- IELTS, TOEFL, JLPT, Goethe, Kana–Kanji, and Ausbildung diagnostics are labelled as KLI readiness/support tools, not official exams.

## Delivery and scoring
- Randomized item and option order.
- Reading, listening, writing, and speaking responses.
- Private speaking/audio storage and expiring listening URLs.
- Weighted blueprint scoring, analytic criteria, dual rating, and adjudication.
- Attempt limits, cooldown, result history, and practice-mode labels.

## Quality assurance
- Content, language, level, bias, technical, and psychometric reviews.
- Two-reviewer gate for content approval.
- One attempt per unique participant for pilot statistics.
- Item difficulty, corrected discrimination, Cronbach alpha, weak-item gate, and validation event history.
- Final validation requires complete content/audio, approved review, n≥30, reliability≥0.70, psychometric approval, and acceptable item statistics.

## Important release boundary
This package is a software and content release candidate. It deliberately does not mark any form as empirically validated. Native/competent-speaker audio, independent expert review, pilot participants, inter-rater calibration, standard setting, and legal approval must be completed in the deployed environment before high-stakes commercial claims are allowed.

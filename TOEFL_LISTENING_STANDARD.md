# KLI Listening Standard

The complete practice form contains 50 KLI-original listening items: 30 short conversations and 20 questions attached to extended conversations or academic talks. Delivery permits two plays per stimulus and uses a fixed US-English voice profile at rate 0.92.

Browser speech synthesis is acceptable for public practice but not for validated high-stakes use. Before changing the form to `validated`, replace every script with studio-recorded audio in private storage, normalize to -1 dBTP, complete linguistic/audio review, and pilot the form. Transcripts remain withheld during delivery.

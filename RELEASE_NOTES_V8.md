# Release Notes V8 — Complete Institutional English Practice

## Complete form

Added `EN-ITP-FULL-PRACTICE`, a 140-item KLI-original commercial practice form:

- Listening Comprehension: 50 items / 35 minutes
- Structure and Written Expression: 40 items / 25 minutes
- Reading Comprehension: 50 items / 55 minutes
- Total: 140 items / 115 minutes

## Listening

- 30 original short-conversation items.
- 20 questions linked to five extended conversations or academic talks.
- Complete scripts, four options, answer keys, competency labels, play limits, voice profile, estimated duration, and timing maps.
- Transcript hidden during delivery.
- Maximum two plays per listening stimulus.

## Scoring and delivery

- Server-side grading and hidden answer keys.
- Section scores for Listening, Structure/Written Expression, and Reading.
- Fixed item and option order for form integrity.
- Section headings, grouped stimuli, global timer, attempt limits, and cooldown.
- Practice-only legal label; no ETS-scaled score conversion or affiliation claim.

## Quality status

The form is suitable for commercial preparation practice. It remains `academic_review` / `content_ready`. Studio recordings and psychometric validation remain required before any high-stakes use.

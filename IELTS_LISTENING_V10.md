# IELTS Listening V10

This release completes the KLI-original IELTS Academic listening practice already seeded in migration 014.

- 4 listening parts and 40 objective questions
- approximately 30 minutes
- British-English browser voice profile
- one-play delivery per part
- transcript withheld during the active test
- visible playback state and mobile-friendly controls
- section-level score reporting

## Deployment

Apply migrations 001–014, then deploy the updated public assets and Netlify functions. Test the selected British English voice in Chrome, Edge, and Safari on the target devices. Browser speech is suitable for practice delivery; use reviewed studio recordings before validated or high-stakes use.

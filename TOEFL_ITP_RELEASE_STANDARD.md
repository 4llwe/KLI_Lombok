# KLI TOEFL ITP Preparation Release Standard

## Publishable now

`EN-ITP-SR-DIAGNOSTIC` is a two-section, 90-item KLI-original commercial practice diagnostic:

- Structure and Written Expression: 40 items / 25 minutes
- Reading Comprehension: 50 items / 55 minutes

It is not a complete TOEFL ITP simulation and must never generate an ETS-equivalent scaled score.

## Source corrections applied

- Corrected *independent of* and *despite being*.
- Removed multi-error and ambiguous Written Expression items.
- Standardized Part A to 15 completion items and Part B to 25 error-identification items.
- Rebuilt all reading questions with four functioning options and KLI-original passages.
- Removed the former institution header and score-equivalence claim.

## Listening gate

The supplied Longman audio is licensed by user attestation, but it is not included in the live form because no matching question booklet, transcript, timestamps, or verified answer key was supplied. The full 140-item simulation remains blocked until that mapping is complete.

## Required human and empirical gates

The form may be sold as practice after internal copy review. It may not be marketed as validated placement/certification evidence until independent content review, bias review, at least 30 unique pilot participants, item statistics, reliability >= .70, and documented standard setting are complete.
